# Setlist

**Setlist and songbook app for guitarists and musicians.**
Add your songs, create setlists and start your gig — even offline.

> **Status:** early development — **[try the live preview](https://besnik.github.io/setlist/app/)**. Today it lists a few sample songs with search; adding your own songs, setlists and offline use are coming next.

## Why Setlist?

Paper songbooks get lost, PDFs on a phone are hard to navigate mid-song, and most music apps need a connection exactly when the venue Wi-Fi fails. Setlist aims to be the one simple place for your songs that just works on stage.

## Planned features

- 🎸 **Songbook** — keep your songs with lyrics and chords in one place.
- 📋 **Setlists** — build setlists for gigs, rehearsals or jam sessions and reorder them in seconds.
- ▶️ **Gig mode** — a distraction-free, stage-friendly view to move from song to song.
- 📴 **Offline first** — everything works without an internet connection.
- 📱 **Installable** — a Progressive Web App (PWA) that runs on phone, tablet and desktop, no app store required.

## Principles

- **Minimal** — does a few things and does them well.
- **Fast** — opens instantly, even on an old phone.
- **Private** — your songs stay on your device.
- **Free and open** — open source under the MIT license.
